I work by embedding directly inside your organization for the length of an engagement, rather than parachuting in for a workshop and disappearing. That means sitting with your team, learning your systems and vendors, and seeing how decisions actually get made — not just writing a policy from the outside.

It also means that when I come back — six months later, a year, two years on — I'm not starting from zero. I already understand how your organization works, so every follow-up engagement moves faster and goes deeper than the last.
