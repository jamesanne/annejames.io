Cookies Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026. The short answer: this site doesn't use cookies, which is why you haven't seen a cookie banner.

No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies, first-party or third-party. It doesn't use local storage, session storage, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or any other technique that stores or reads information on your device to recognise you.

No analytics

There are no analytics, counters or tracking pixels on this site. The Privacy Policy explains what little data the site does involve.

How the menus work

The mobile menu and dropdowns work with ordinary page code while you're on the page. Nothing is saved once you leave.

No embedded third-party content

There are no embedded videos, social-media widgets, chat tools, maps or ads, and fonts are served from this site rather than an external font service. Links to Substack, LinkedIn and meetergo take you to those sites, which set their own cookies under their own policies once you're there.

If this changes

If I ever add something that needs cookies, I'll list it here and ask for your consent before it runs. I won't use pre-ticked boxes or banners designed to wear you down.
