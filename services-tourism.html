Personalization builds loyalty. It also collects exactly the kind of data that turns into a breach headline.

I help tourism boards, hotel groups, and travel platforms use AI for personalization, pricing, and guest communication without crossing lines your guests — or your regulators — care about.

Where this usually starts

A recommendation engine that knows a little too much, a dynamic pricing model nobody can fully explain, or an AI concierge that's started making promises the property can't keep.

What tends to go wrong without governance

Pricing that looks discriminatory

Dynamic pricing models optimize for revenue and end up charging different guests different prices in ways that look — or are — unfair, with no one able to explain why.

Guest data drift

Personalization tools quietly expand what data they collect and how long it's kept, past what guests agreed to and past what local privacy law allows.

AI that overpromises

A chatbot or concierge tool confirms an amenity, refund, or booking detail that isn't actually true — and the brand is on the hook for what it said.

What the engagement covers

Personalization & recommendations

A review of what guest data feeds your recommendation and loyalty systems, and where the line sits between helpful and unsettling.

Dynamic pricing

Governance for pricing algorithms so outcomes can be explained and defended — to guests, to partners, and to regulators.

Guest-facing AI

Chatbots, concierge tools, and AI-written communications, with clear boundaries on what they're allowed to promise and an escalation path when they get it wrong.

Cross-border data & privacy

Practical guidance for brands operating across jurisdictions with different rules on guest data, consent, and automated decision-making.

Let's map what your systems are already doing

Most travel and hospitality brands have more AI in production than any one person has fully mapped. That's a good place to start.
