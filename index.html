Field observation 002

Why pipelines leak

Most go-to-market loads the top of the funnel. The problem is, deals die at the bottom. Maybe pricing doesn't match what the buyer expected, or the proposal can't get through procurement because no one mapped the internal politics or anticipated the DPIA scrutiny. At that stage, you don't win on features but on trust, earned by understanding your buyer's specific world. When a prospect walks away at proposal stage, they rarely come back. This is why I anticipate risks at the bottom of the funnel before I feed the top.
