How the work goes

  1. 1

    Assess

    A working audit of where AI already touches your organization — tools, vendors, and the decisions people are quietly making without a policy to guide them.

  2. 2

    Design

    A governance framework sized to your organization: clear roles, review thresholds, and documentation that people will actually use.

  3. 3

    Implement

    Policy written in plain language, staff training, and review workflows built into the tools your team already uses.

  4. 4

    Sustain

    Ongoing advisory as your tools, vendors, and the regulatory landscape change — so governance keeps pace instead of falling behind.

Governance isn't a brake on innovation. It's what lets you move fast without breaking public trust.
